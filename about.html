Our Story

Built by hand,
meant to outlast trend.

Nostra began in 2019 in a small workshop with three leatherworkers and one belief: that the things we carry every day deserve more care than they usually get.

Every bag, shoe and accessory is still cut, stitched and finished by hand — no assembly lines, no shortcuts. We'd rather make fewer things well than more things fast.

Material First

We source full-grain leather and waxed canvas that only gets better with wear.

Made Slow

Small batches, hand-stitched, checked piece by piece before it ships.

Repair, Not Replace

Every Nostra piece comes with free repairs for life. We want it to last.
